Dear Lalisara,

You passed. Read that again, slowly: you passed.

Out of everyone who applied, everyone who hoped, everyone who walked into that room carrying the very same dream, Etihad Airways looked at you and said yes.

Here is the truth, though: this is not luck, and it is not a surprise. It is the result of every early morning and every late night, of every time you practised one more answer when you were already tired, and of every time you chose to keep believing when it would have been easier to stop. We saw how much you wanted this. We saw how hard you worked for it. And now the rest of the world gets to see what we have always known: that you were made for this.

Soon there will be a uniform with your name on it, wings on your chest, and a cabin full of people who feel a little safer and a little more at home simply because you are there. You will carry your kindness to 40,000 feet. You will watch sunrises that most people only ever dream about.

And on the days that are long, and the nights that are longer, please remember exactly how today feels: the moment your dream finally said yes back.

Be proud of yourself today. Truly, deeply proud. We certainly are.

Blue skies and tailwinds, always.
